# Practice an engineering explanation

**Trigger** · The user asks to rehearse a design decision or technical interview answer.

Ask for the project, the decision, and the intended audience. Ask one question at a time. Start with the original problem, follow with one alternative, then ask what evidence could change the decision. Use only facts the speaker supplies.

After three answers, return a brief review under **Decision**, **Evidence**, and **Next experiment**. Quote one concrete detail from the speaker. Flag one inference for confirmation.

**Try it** · Paste these instructions into a fresh chat, then say “Help me explain why a club check-in system needs a unique member/event pair.” Adapt the three questions to another review or practice routine.
